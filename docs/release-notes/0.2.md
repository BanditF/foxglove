# Foxglove 0.2

*Draft, from the pull requests merged to main since `release/0.1` branched (#216 onward). The
release agent finalises it when 0.2 is tagged. Some fixes here also went to 0.1.x.*

0.2 turns the Plugins tab into a working plugin system, with its first plugins, and makes the
app and firmware quieter: nothing runs on a timer that an event could start instead.

## Highlights

- **Plugins you can use.** Installed plugins run in a sandbox, with only the permissions you
  approve, and can now:
  - hold the lights for a while and give them back cleanly (**lighting leases**), so a
    notification wins over a colour sync and the lights come back as they were;
  - draw on the left panel, react to keys you bind (F13–F24), keep passwords in Windows'
    credential store, and have settings in the Plugins tab;
  - reach the network only where their permissions say, through Foxglove.
- **First plugins:**
  - **Pomodoro:** a focus timer on the left panel and the underglow, with keys to pause and
    restart. It survives restarts and sleep.
  - **Mic mute:** red and MUTED while your microphone is muted, and a key to toggle it.
  - **Broadcast Status:** OBS recording or streaming as REC or LIVE, with the scene name.
  - **OpenRGB (E1.31):** now holds the lights with a low-priority lease, so notifications still
    show.
  - **Corsair iCUE:** the underglow mirrors a Corsair device's colours (bring Corsair's SDK).
  - **Foxglove for SignalRGB:** SignalRGB drives the underglow through Foxglove's E1.31
    receiver, under the same low-priority lease.
- **Scenes (#487).** A new **Scenes** page, after Lights, runs light across the room: a map of
  your monitors, case, keyboard and mouse (placed from Windows' display layout, drag a device to
  where it stands) with a live preview, six effects (scanline, sweep, ripple, breathe, gradient,
  drift) with colour or palette, speed, direction and width, **Start** and **Stop**, and presets,
  built in or your own. Each profile keeps its own scene. The case gets a colour per zone; the
  keyboard gets a colour per half on firmware with per-LED frames (dev builds for now), and one
  colour for both halves otherwise.
- **Scenes for the Bandit wallpapers (#488).** Twelve built-in scenes, one per Bandit wallpaper,
  in its colours and motion (the HUD's scan, the radar's sweep, FURY's ink rings, the hearth's
  embers, the turning wheel). The wallpaper on screen is marked **On screen**, and **Right
  screen too** shows the wallpaper's right-screen art while its scene runs.
- **Scene triggers (#489).** A Triggers card on the Scenes page: **Start with this profile**
  (so a game's profile brings its scene, and leaving it ends the scene), **Ripple on key press**
  (a ring from the key you press; Foxglove sees the key's place, never what it types) and
  **Sweep on layer change** (one sweep in the layer's overlay colour).
- **Lighting presets.** Curated underglow looks that match the Bandit wallpapers, with matching
  right-screen art if you want it.
- **Art in one place (#515).** The Screens page shows what both screens show now, what the
  keyboard keeps ("Kept: 5 of 8 places") with pictures of the pieces that come with it, and
  every piece on this PC once with one badge. **Keep on keyboard** is one click and asks what to
  replace when the keyboard is full; Rotation steps through the keyboard's own art; one strip
  says what is being sent and, when something fails, what to do. The Art library window is gone.
  The old hotlist is no longer stepped: Rotation is what the keyboard holds, so keep a piece on
  the keyboard to add it.
- **The tray's Screens part (#519)** shows both screens, ‹ / Next › through the keyboard's art,
  Art… and the same sending strip; Push file… left the tray. The left screen has a **Change…**
  picker with previews, and after a firmware update Screens says what happened to the kept and
  built-in art; a pinned built-in follows its name across updates (#521).
- **Art.**
  - **Art library view:** browse built-in, saved and your own animations.
  - **Art follows the layer or preset**, and `&art_next` / `&art_prev` step the right screen's
    art from a key.
- **More keyboards.**
  - **Board profiles:** a new board is a profile file plus firmware, without app changes. The
    Eyelash Corne is built in. The Toucan2 and Halcyon Corne e-paper profiles are there but
    untested.
  - **Toucan2:** a PC-drawn panel on its 144×168 screen.
  - **VIA keyboards** (such as the Keychron K5 Pro), read-only, with a per-keyboard **Pause
    Foxglove** so VIA or the maker's app can use it.
- **Battery.** The backlight turns off while idle, the underglow dims on battery (each half by
  its own cable and charge: 100% on USB, 80% on battery, very weak below 20%), the right half
  transmits at lower power, and the keyboard sleeps after 30 minutes idle.

## Features

- **PC load on the left screen (#506):** a fourth choice under the left screen's **Change…**:
  CPU, GPU and memory use as three bars with numbers, from Windows' performance counters
  (Task Manager's figures). Sampled about once a second only while that layout is drawn; the
  counters close when it stops showing, Foxglove pauses, or the PC is locked or its screen is
  off. Only changed rows go to the keyboard. App-only: the left screen is drawn by the PC.
- **Device batteries (#504):** the Keyboard page's **Devices** card and the tray panel list the
  batteries of connected Bluetooth devices (from Windows' Bluetooth battery property, followed
  by a device watcher) and game controllers (`Windows.Gaming.Input`), with a low-battery
  notification like the halves'. Controllers have no battery event: they're read when they
  arrive and at most once a minute while the page or the tray panel is open.
- **Save battery: dim lights on battery** on the Lights page, on by default: each half's
  underglow shows your brightness on USB, 80% of it on battery and very weak below 20% charge,
  with the halves' state in a line ("Right half on battery · 80%"). **Adjust…** sets the three
  levels and the threshold. The keyboard does the dimming and keeps the levels, so it works
  without the app.
- **Left-half battery:** firmware with the LEFT_BATTERY bit reports the left's own level (on
  CONN_STATE, sent again when it changes); the status strip, tray tooltip and low-battery notices
  show both halves.
- **Update keyboard** (Keyboard page, Firmware card, #382): pick a folder with new firmware and
  Foxglove updates both halves, left first, with the flasher's checks (each half's drive by its USB
  serial, the image's half marker, a read-back of the new build). It says what will happen first,
  shows each step, and refuses a Bluetooth-only left or a nearly flat right half.
- **Start with Windows** is a switch in App -> Startup and updates, on by default. It uses the
  same Run entry as the installer's option, and stays off once you turn it off.
- **Plugin API 1.3** (`docs/local-api.md`):
  - **Lighting:**
    - leases with low, normal and high priority (#250);
    - restore checked on readback, including speed, with the snapshot kept until it takes
      (#317);
    - leases changed in place, and the E1.31 receiver holds a lease (#330);
    - waits for a split keyboard's other half to catch up (#261).
    - the Plugins tab says who holds the lights: "Lights: Mic mute (high), under OpenRGB
      (low)", or "Lights: yours" (#376).
  - **Left panel:** overrides held until replaced or released (#310, #255).
  - **Key actions** bound to F13–F24 (#241).
  - **Per-plugin secrets** in Windows' credential store (#249).
  - **Plugin settings,** including a random pairing token (#324).
  - **Network through Foxglove:**
    - scoped `net.connect` and `net.listen` (#232);
    - loopback TCP listening, plus the SDK's WebSocket server side (#323).
  - **Facts:**
    - the microphone as `mic.*`, with mute through Foxglove (#251);
    - media as facts (#235);
    - `keyboard.layer`, `keyboard.right.battery`, `keyboard.link` and more (#321).
  - **Waiting for an app:** `apps.watch` tells a plugin when an app's window opens or closes
    (#296).
- **Plugin catalog v2,** with publishing through GitHub Releases and Pages (#217, #219).
- **New look** (#384, first step of the UI overhaul #393): dark graphite with Bandit orange,
  Chakra Petch headings, IBM Plex Sans and Mono text (bundled, SIL OFL). Theme picks Dark,
  Light or System for the windows; the layer overlay keeps its look.
- **Settings in pages** (#385): a sidebar with Keyboard, Lights, Screens, Plugins and Settings,
  the profile picker at its foot, and a status strip for both halves (connection, firmware,
  link, battery, Bluetooth profile, and a warning when unplugging would leave the keyboard
  unable to type). The window remembers the last page.
- **Keyboard page** (#386): the board at the top (click a screen or the lights to open that
  page), a Firmware card with "Ask the keyboard again", an Actions card grouped by kind, and
  the link to this PC in plain words ("Left half: on USB", "Right: no reply yet").
- **Lights page** (#387): effect pills, colour swatches with a custom hue and saturation,
  brightness and speed sliders (speed is new in the app), the accent and right-half switches,
  Apply colour, and both halves' state with Refresh. The presets card waits for #306.
- **Screens page** (#388): the left screen's card (live preview, Drive, Pause, Show, game
  hints) beside the right screen's art: what's playing, the library as small 1-bit thumbnails
  (each piece once, no unreadable rows), Push file..., "Saved on the keyboard: 1 of 8" with
  the slots behind a click, and the hotlist as pills with Next.
- **Plugins page** (#389): one card per plugin, three to a row, with its tag (CATALOG,
  INSTALLED or SIDELOADED), On, Settings and Remove; plugins that run outside the sandbox say
  so on the card. "Install from file…" sits in the page header, and the catalog's state reads
  "Catalog: up to date" with Check now. Catalog plugins show Install greyed out until their
  downloads exist.
- **Settings page** (#390): a grid of cards. Layer overlay (size, label font scale, fitting,
  legend mode, display duration and delay, distance from the edge, alignment, the display, then
  the font colour and each layer's Show, Hold and colour) on the left; Profiles (editing,
  switch by program, import and export), Theme and updates (Dark / Light / System, the version
  and Check now) and Other keyboards (VIA) on the right.
- **One name per thing** (#393, the names and places review): the Settings page is now **App**, so
  there are no settings inside settings, and each thing has one name everywhere: the Keyboard
  page's **Connection** (was Link to this PC), **Key actions** (Actions), **Keymap source** (the
  overlay's Connection), **Refresh** (Ask the keyboard again), **Update firmware…** (Update
  keyboard…) and **Bluetooth slot** (Bluetooth profile, also in the status strip); on Lights,
  **Keyboard** (Underglow), **Presets** (Lighting presets), **Refresh** (Refresh lights) and
  **Other devices** (PC & peripherals) with **Built-in support** and **Windows lighting**; on
  Screens, the left screen's **On** (Drive left screen) and **App hints** (Game hints); a
  plugin's **Options** (Settings); the default profile is **Default** (Base settings) in the
  window, the tray and the CLI; on App, **Appearance** and **Startup and updates** (were one
  Theme and updates card), **Layer colours** with **Text colour**, and **Other keyboards**,
  whose **Refresh** (Rescan) and **Let other apps use it** (Pause Foxglove for this keyboard)
  say what they do. Copy diagnostics says **Board type**. Config files and keys are unchanged.
- **Tray panel** (#391): a left click on the tray icon opens a small panel next to it, on
  whichever edge the taskbar is: both halves, profile pills, the lights (on, effect, colour,
  brightness), the right art with Next and Push file..., Pause left panel, and "Open Foxglove →"
  with the update notice. It closes on Esc, a click elsewhere or when it loses focus; a click
  elsewhere also reaches the window it lands on (#405). A right click still opens the menu.
- **Tray icon** (#403): the Bandit fox glyphs from the app icon (#299), drawn for each
  small-icon size (16, 20, 24 and 32 px), white on a dark taskbar and black on a light one. Solid
  with a keyboard connected, the outline without, and greyed with an orange pause badge while
  Foxglove is paused.
- **Sidebar mark:** the settings window's sidebar shows the Bandit fox in orange, the same
  glyph as the tray icon, instead of the arrowhead.
- **Pause Foxglove** (#404), from the tray panel or the tray menu: Foxglove leaves the keyboard
  alone until you resume. Plugin lighting leases give the lights back (the keyboard shows its
  own effects), the left panel goes back to the firmware, the right art and the Windows-accent
  colour stay as they are, and the layer overlay hides; the tray icon shows the paused glyph.
  Resuming brings it all back. A restart starts unpaused.
- **Board drawing** (#392): the Keyboard page draws the board from its profile (a new
  `[drawing]` table: columns, rows, thumb keys and column stagger), with the left panel's
  picture and the right screen's art in the screens and the underglow in its colour. Clicking
  a screen opens Screens; clicking the underglow opens Lights.
- **Plugins tab:** icons, an empty state, the app icon and tray glyphs (#206, #299).
- **Lighting presets** and matching right-screen art (#265, #273).
- **Art:**
  - cycling triggers (layer, preset, key) (#300, #302);
  - the library view and source folders (#303, #308).
- **Right underglow:** gets the PC's colour over the relay, acknowledged, newest wins (#269).
- **Board profiles** (#339), **Toucan2 panel** (#216), and **wide or tall panels** with
  `ROWS_AT`, reserved screen formats shown as unsupported (#294, #337).
- **VIA keyboards,** stage 1 (#325):
  - no switch-matrix reads, Vial refused;
  - "Active layer: unknown";
  - pause per keyboard.
  - Keyboards without a reliable USB serial are told apart by where they're plugged in, with a
    **This is the same keyboard** confirmation (#326, #328).
- **Settings in a window of its own:** the Settings window is a normal app window now, not a
  panel inside the overlay. Drag it by its title bar to any display; it opens where you left it
  (centred on a display that's still there if its display is gone), shows on the taskbar, and
  nothing else on screen is greyed out or blocked while it's open. The layer overlay still
  previews on its display, and clicking a key there still opens the keymap editor.
  - It keeps its size when dragged between displays with different scaling (say 100% and 150%)
    instead of resizing back and forth at the border, and it remembers its place in the
    display's own scale.
  - Minimizing it closes it, like the X: the app lives in the tray anyway. The overlay preview
    and an overlay drag end with it, and it opens again from the tray at its place.
- **Drag the layer overlay:** with Settings open, drag the key popup anywhere, onto either
  display; it stays there (App → Layer overlay → Position says so, with **Reset**).
  Choosing a Display or an Alignment puts it back at that display's anchor.
  - The drag carries on across displays: when the pointer reaches the next display, the
    overlay moves there, at that display's scaling (say 100% to 150%), and keeps following;
    let go and it stays whole on that display. Its place is remembered in the display's own
    scale.
- **Overlay opacity:** App → Layer overlay → **Opacity** makes the key popup see-through,
  from 100% all the way down to 0%. It applies at once and is saved per profile. A fresh
  config starts at 20%; a saved one keeps its own value (100% if it predates the slider).
  - The sliders run the row's width. A tick marks 20%: dragging within 4% of it snaps there
    (hold **Alt** to pass it), and the arrow keys step 1%.
  - While you drag a slider the overlay shows as a live preview; it goes when you let go or
    close Settings.
  - At 0% the overlay is hidden, and a faint outline shows where it is whenever it would show:
    for a layer, while previewed, or while you drag it.
  - **Key labels** has its own slider, which follows Opacity until you move it; **Follow HUD**
    links it again. Faint keys with clear labels (or the other way round) work too.
- **Firmware power:** idle backlight off, 0 dBm right half, battery dim by power state (D7 0E:
  100% on USB, 80% on battery, 12% below 20% charge, saved on both halves), deep sleep after
  30 min, a slower display tick (#220–#223, #290).

## Fixes

- **The host link's route is reported right:** with the left half on USB and Bluetooth at
  once, the log and the Lights page said "BLE" whenever a Bluetooth interface was listed; they
  now name the interface the link opened (USB when it answers).
- **"Save battery" no longer says "needs newer keyboard firmware" after a lost reply:** a read
  that goes unanswered (a USB/Bluetooth switch) is asked once more, then shows "the keyboard
  didn't answer"; only firmware without the feature gets the firmware hint.
- **"Both halves now" reads the right half fresh:** a stale right half is asked once more, and
  its age keeps counting while the page is open.
- **No false "battery very low" after a flash or reconnect:** the keyboard reports 0% until
  its first battery reading; that is now "no reading", not a level, so it can't raise or
  re-arm the low-battery notice.
- **The right half's battery shows over USB too:** its first reading comes after the keyboard
  connects, and the app now takes it (and every later change); the diagnostics list both halves.
- **Installs for you, without UAC:** the MSI is now per user, in
  `%LOCALAPPDATA%\Programs\Foxglove`, so installs and updates need no administrator prompt.
  **Start with Windows** is the app's switch alone (on by default; Windows' Startup apps page
  shows it too), and uninstalling removes it. **Coming from 0.1:** uninstall Foxglove 0.1 in
  Settings › Apps first (your settings stay); the new installer says so if you don't (#444).

- **The app finds the keyboard when it is on USB and Bluetooth at once,** with 0.1 firmware
  too. It talks to whichever connection answers instead of always taking USB, and it looks for
  the keyboard again each time a connection comes up. Before, after a PC restart the app could
  show "Left not connected · Right not connected" while the keyboard worked.
- **Settings opens compact:** about 880 x 600 at the normal text size, centred on the display
  under the mouse, and never more than 70% of that display. A size you set is remembered and
  wins.
- **Nothing overlaps at the compact size.** The Lights page's cards sit side by side only when
  both fit and stack otherwise; "Both halves now" no longer draws over the Underglow card, and
  its Left and Right labels no longer draw over their values. Rows with buttons at the right
  (Apply colour, the PC lights' Identify, Off and Calibrate…) wrap their text beside the
  buttons instead of running under them, and the page's scroll bar has its own gutter.
- **The settings window keeps its width when the keyboard connects or is replugged.** Long
  firmware and link lines on the Keyboard page wrap, and a status chip too long for the strip
  is cut with "…" (its hover has the whole text).
- **Firmware releases ship the settings-reset UF2,** `foxglove-<version>-settings-reset.uf2`, listed in
  SHA256SUMS (#378). It is the last resort for a half that hangs (RESCUE.md): it **erases that
  half's saved settings and its Bluetooth pairings**. Flash the half's own UF2 again after it,
  then pair your devices again.
- **Lights:**
  - retried when the keyboard is busy (#224);
  - plugins can change them before the overlay opens the keymap (#203);
  - restored with speed and brightness, and retried on the next connect if a restore didn't
    take (#317).
- **OpenRGB:** each colour confirmed on the keyboard, sequence numbers per sender (#244, #247).
- **Wallpaper Engine is optional, and found anywhere:** in any Steam library (a second drive
  too), through Steam's own records, or a custom folder. Without it, "Match the wallpaper" and
  the left panel's wallpaper animation follow your Windows desktop wallpaper, and the Art page
  shows "Works best with Wallpaper Engine" instead of an error (#446).
- **Sharp at any display scaling:** every build now carries an application manifest
  (per-monitor DPI, long paths, Windows 10/11, modern controls), so the tray menu, file dialogs
  and message boxes render crisply at 150% too (#447).
- **The left panel's clock and date follow your region settings:** 12- or 24-hour (with a
  small AM/PM mark), your date order, and day and month names in your language. Changing them
  in Windows shows on the panel right away (#445).
- **Plugin runner:** notices a plugin's exit even when its output stays open (#240). A stopped
  plugin no longer leaves its thread and registration behind (#296).
- **Plugin access:** outside tools are never granted plugin secrets (#271), and the OpenRGB
  catalog entry asks for its port, not unrestricted network (#322).
- **Tray panel follows the keyboard:** after Next (or any built-in art choice) the panel and
  the Screens page name the art now on the right screen, not the one before it. Lights switched
  on or off from the keyboard show in the panel too, and while Foxglove is paused its title dims
  and says PAUSED next to the Resume Foxglove button.
- **Tray menu, slimmed:** a right click on the tray icon offers Open Foxglove, Pause Foxglove
  (Resume Foxglove while paused), Profile ▸, Open log folder and Quit. The right art, the left
  screen's pause and updates are in the tray panel (left click) and the window
  (`docs/images/tray-menu.png`).
- **Board profile over BLE:** ZMK names the keyboard `Eyelash_Corne` over BLE; the profile now
  matches product names by letters and digits only, in any case, so the Eyelash Corne's
  profile (panel, keymap, lighting) is found on either link. "Keyboard connected" is logged
  once per connect, not twice.
- **Tray tooltip battery:** the hover tooltip shows the battery again. Its status line carried
  the art relay's whole report, which pushed the battery line past Windows' 127 characters; it
  now says Connected, Disconnected or Paused, and only the status is ever cut.
- **Low battery** (#372, #373): a Windows notification when a half drops below 15%, and again
  below 5%, once each until it charges past 20% (a switch on the Keyboard page turns it off).
  The tray icon's tooltip shows the battery (`Right 61% · Left —`, `—` for a half that isn't
  reporting), from the same reading as the tray panel, as soon as the keyboard connects.
  Only the right half reports its level today.
- **Copy diagnostics** (#371), in the settings window's status strip: one click copies a
  report for a bug report. It has the app and firmware versions, the connection, the HELLO
  capabilities, plugins and who holds the lights, Windows and the GPU, and the last 200 log
  lines. Your user name, PC name and profile path are replaced, and passwords and tokens are
  masked.
  - Its Connection line names both links: how the keyboard types to this PC and how Foxglove's
    host link reaches it, such as `USB (typing) · host link BLE` (USB Raw HID is silent before
    firmware #434). The status strip and the tray panel word the left half the same way.
- **Log file** (#370): Foxglove's messages and any crash report now go to
  `%APPDATA%\Foxglove\logs\foxglove.log` with a timestamp (it rolls to `foxglove.1.log` at 1 MB,
  about 2 MB at most), with anything that looks like a password or token masked. **Open log
  folder** in the tray menu opens it.
- **Starting before the taskbar:** started at sign-in before Explorer's taskbar is up, Foxglove
  keeps running and adds its tray icon when the taskbar appears, and again after Explorer
  restarts. The log now opens first, so every start (with how long after boot), a second
  launch, a failed start and a GPU-reset restart each leave a line.
- **Keyboard connected in the log:** the log said only "Waiting for a keyboard with the host
  link"; now each connection adds "Keyboard connected (USB)" or "(BLE)" with the keyboard's name.
- **GPU resets** (#346): when the graphics driver resets or is updated under Foxglove (NVIDIA
  logs "The GPU has been disconnected" for it), Foxglove hands the screens back and starts a
  fresh copy of itself, so the overlay and windows don't stay frozen. It does this at most 3
  times in 10 minutes.
- **Flasher:** never prompts a double tap while a hands-free entry may still land (#205).
- **MSI:**
  - a rebuilt same-version MSI replaces the installed one;
  - tag builds set the MSI version correctly (they passed `--version`, which built nothing);
  - the plugin host builds on Linux (#309, #319).

## No more polling

Nothing in the app, the plugins or the firmware runs on a free timer any more. Each poll that
remains is started by an event, stopped by one, and bounded (#278, #283):

- **Plugin host and SDK:**
  - no heartbeat or silence watchdog; exits come from the process handle (#310);
  - panels are held, not re-sent;
  - OpenRGB blocks on frames, and Pomodoro doesn't tick while paused (#310).
- **App** (#311):
  - **Panel keep-alive:** none when the firmware holds the panel (`PANEL_HOLD`, #285).
  - **Preview:** it repaints on a new frame.
  - **Reconnect:** it waits for the device to arrive, after a bounded burst.
  - **Crashing services:** they stop after 5 failures.
- **Idle wake-ups:** repaints come from the workers, and the ZMK and UDP readers block (#316).
- **Idle CPU** (#410):
  - **Away:** while the PC is locked or its display is off, the left screen goes back to the
    keyboard's own, and the art hotlist's timer and QMK's layer renewal stop. They start again
    when you're back.
  - **Keyboard gone:** the link tries 3 times, then waits for the keyboard to arrive or for its
    Bluetooth to connect. It no longer looks for it every 3 s.
  - **Paused media:** a long title stands still instead of scrolling.
  - **Apps watch:** the window hook is on only while a plugin waits for an app.
- **OBS:** connects when OBS's window appears, not on a timer (#296).
- **PC & peripherals lighting** (Lights page, #423): Foxglove drives the HP OMEN 45L case
  lights (diamond display, interior light, three front fans) with a colour per zone, Identify,
  Off and Match keyboard, so OMEN Light Studio can go. It also lists Dynamic Lighting devices
  (coming soon) and the OMEN liquid cooler (not controllable yet), and warns while Light Studio
  runs. New boards are entries in `crates/devices/lighting-catalog.toml`.
- **What each device can do:** the PC & peripherals card shows each device's capabilities as
  chips (colour per zone or per LED with lamp counts, effects, brightness, screens with size,
  depth, and images, animations or live frames), from the catalog, a LampArray's own attributes
  and the keyboard's HELLO. The controls follow them.
- **Add a device…** for bespoke lighting gear: pick an unrecognised device, try a known
  protocol, name its zones one click at a time (each test blinks one zone and puts it back),
  and save it as your own catalog entry, which you can copy to share.
- **Colour calibrator** (Lights page, "Calibrate…" on the keyboard or a PC light): make a device
  and your screen (or another device, such as the keyboard) look alike. Seven mid-range test
  colours (white, grey, red, green, blue, skin, warm; none at full strength, so there is room
  both ways): for each, adjust the **Light** (what the device is sent), the **Screen** (what
  Foxglove draws for that device) or both with the "Adjust" switch, then click **These match**.
  Each side keeps its own values (say blue down 30% on the light, up 5% on the screen), and
  colours between your matches follow them. The matches are listed with Redo and Remove. A
  picked colour far from every match shows "not matched yet"; click it to add a match. The
  calibration belongs to the device (or one zone of it), never to a colour or a profile.
  Calibrations from before matches load unchanged. Every colour Foxglove sends, from the
  underglow, the case or a plugin, goes through it, and every swatch and picker for the device
  is drawn through its screen side.

## For developers

- **Code review:** blocks free-running polls (#278, #283).
- **Test lab:** cases for leases, Pomodoro, the microphone, secrets, OBS (stand-in and real) and
  art keys. Also RGB latency, and lamp and frame-dot tools for the LampArray sitting (#242,
  #262, #270, #275, #284, #286, #304, #307, #333).
- **Simulator:** checks for deep sleep, the battery dim cap, the panel stream, `PANEL_HOLD`,
  `ROWS_AT` and art keys (#267, #268, #327, #334).
- **Disk hygiene:** slim debug info, plus `scripts/clean-worktrees.ps1` (#312, #329).
- **User guide:** presets, art cycling, the art library, plugins and VIA (#332), and "If
  something goes wrong": recovery after a flash, soft off, stuck lights, GPU resets and
  reporting a problem (#375).
- **This PC's values in one place:** the halves' serials, the flasher's rollback folder, the
  workspace and worktree folders come from `tools/local.toml` (or environment variables), with
  this PC's values as the defaults; `tools/local.example.toml` lists them.
- **Scenes core (#482, #483, #484):** `crates/scene` places every monitor, keyboard half,
  mouse and case zone in one room map in millimetres from Windows' display layout (no setup),
  runs one clock that can align to a wallpaper's epoch, and has six effects (scanline, sweep,
  ripple, breathe, gradient, noise) as pure functions. "Copy diagnostics" lists the room map.
- **Scene output (#485):** a running scene drives the OMEN case (a colour per zone) and the
  keyboard (one colour for the board) through the existing drivers and calibration, about 15
  case frames and 10 keyboard colours a second, and only on change. It holds the keyboard under
  a low lease (notifications show over it) and gives the lights back when it stops; it draws
  nothing while you are away or Foxglove is paused. Until the Scenes page, only a hidden demo
  for live checks starts one: launch with `FOXGLOVE_SCENE_DEMO=<seconds>`. "Copy
  diagnostics" has a Scene line.
- **Per-LED frames, SET_FRAME (#272):** dev firmware takes per-LED colours for both halves from
  the PC (D7 0C, HELLO bit 14). The left draws each frame at once; the right gets only the LEDs
  that changed over the split link, at most 10 times a second, and nothing for a static frame.
  Each half's battery dim applies on top. The keyboard's own effect comes back on RELEASE, 30 s
  without a frame, a USB unplug or an output change. LEDs are numbered in strip order (0-20
  left, 21-41 right) until the LED-order sitting maps them to keys. Digitalis has `set_frame` /
  `release_frame`; GA firmware keeps one colour, and the scene output moves to frames in #529.
- **Effects on the keyboard itself, SET_EFFECT (#490):** dev firmware takes a scanline, sweep,
  ripple or breathe once (D7 0F, HELLO bit 19) and each half draws it from its LEDs' places
  and the scene's clock, with `crates/scene`'s maths (the simulator holds the firmware to its
  vectors, `crates/scene/tests/keyboard_effect_vectors.rs`). Nothing streams: the right gets its
  copy over the split link once per change, its clock re-anchored to the left's on each send,
  and the effect runs on with the app closed until RELEASE, a SET_FRAME or a reboot. PLACE says
  where the halves sit in the room. Each half goes dark while idle and its battery dim applies.
  The LED places are provisional until the LED-order sitting. Digitalis has `set_effect` /
  `place_effect` / `release_effect`; the scene output doesn't use it yet.
- **Scene output on frames (#529):** with HELLO bit 14 the scene sends the keyboard SET_FRAME
  frames, each half its own colour (`scene_output::KeyboardFrame`), still at most 10 a second
  and only on change, through each half's colour calibration (zones `left` / `right`). The
  scene's lease keeps the frame's mix as its colour, and the lease arbiter hands the frames back
  (RELEASE) before anything else shows: a notification lease, pause, the scene's end and quit;
  the scene hands them back itself while you are away. The scene draws again when its lease
  shows again, and after the keyboard's own hand-back (the D7 8C event, a still scene's 30 s
  timeout), never on a timer. Per-LED colours wait for the LED-order sitting: the Corne's profile
  has a provisional `led_keys` map that scenes use only once its `led_map` says `checked`.
  Colours you pick on the Lights page while a scene (or a notification) holds the keyboard are
  what comes back when it ends. Firmware without the bit keeps the one colour. "Copy
  diagnostics" says which the keyboard got.
- **Scenes page (#487):** `scenes_ui` (the page, drawn from a `View` with intents) and
  `scene_looks` (the six effects' parameters and the built-in presets).
- **Scene triggers (#489):** `scene_triggers` (pure rules: `on_profile`, `key_point`, the ring
  and sweep) and `crates/scene` `burst` (one-shot rings and sweeps over a scene, at most four,
  drawn by the scene's own frames). Profile switches go through `scenes_ui::profile_changed`;
  key reports come from KeyPeek's reader (`keyboard.rs`, row and column only) and layer changes
  from the `keyboard.layer` fact. No new hooks or timers. The three boxes are in the profiled
  `[scenes]` section (`start_with_profile`, `ripple_keys`, `sweep_layers`).
- **Wallpaper scenes (#488):** `app/resources/scenes.json` (format `foxglove-scenes` v1) holds
  the wallpapers' palettes and looks and their right-screen art (built-in set, else the
  wallpaper's library file, else the copy bundled for the lighting presets, else
  `Bandit_Emblem`); a test checks every colour, range and name. The art rides the art service's
  preset slot (`scene_run::set_art`) and is handed back when the scene ends. `wallpaper.rs`
  keeps the ids on screen for the page's "On screen", set by its folder watch. The chosen look is the profiled `[scenes]` section; saved presets are
  `[scene_presets]`; drags stay in `[scene]`. Start, Stop and edits while running go through
  `scene_run` (`set_effect` swaps the look in place), so the scheduler, pacing and leases are
  #485's. The preview draws only while the page is shown, at 20 frames a second at most, and
  not while minimised or away. The demo launch variable still works.
- **Wallpaper sync, stage 1 (#493, #377):** with Wallpaper Engine running and a Bandit
  wallpaper on a monitor, Foxglove tells that wallpaper the keyboard's layer, whether game mode
  (a GAME layer) is on, and the running scene's clock and geometry, through Wallpaper Engine's own
  command line (`-control applyProperties`), once per change and never streamed. The wallpaper
  draws the scene's scanline in step with the keys and the case from the shared epoch (bandit-
  wallpapers #494: `hud` and `bandit-radar`). Without Wallpaper Engine, or while it isn't running,
  nothing is sent. Quitting Foxglove tells the wallpapers it has gone. Shared test vectors
  (`crates/scene/tests/wallpaper_vectors.json`) keep the Rust and JavaScript maths identical.
- **Versions:** 0.2.0 for the app and the crates; plugins keep their own versions. MSI versions
  keep increasing past 0.1.x.
